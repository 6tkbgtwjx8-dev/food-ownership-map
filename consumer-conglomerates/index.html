Inclusion rules

  • Scope: companies whose revenue is predominantly branded consumer packaged goods: food, beverages incl. alcohol, household & personal care/beauty, tobacco. Pure retailers excluded.
  • Meat & dairy: major branded protein and dairy processors included (JBS, Tyson, MBRF, WH Group, Lactalis). Pure commodity traders/processors excluded (see below).
  • Revenue measure: reported revenue/net sales, net of excise taxes where companies report both (tobacco, beer, spirits). Continuing operations only (Unilever excludes demerged ice cream).
  • Diversified firms: ranked on consumer-goods revenue where segments are reported (Henkel’s consumer segment alone is too small to qualify). Majority-owned listed subsidiaries are not double-counted (Pilgrim’s→JBS, Smithfield→WH Group, Ambev→AB InBev, Suntory Beverage & Food→Suntory).
  • Private companies: included only with published or company-stated revenue (Mars, Lactalis, Ferrero, Suntory).

2025–26 portfolio changes reflected

  • Mars + Kellanova: closed 11 Dec 2025; Mars now describes itself as a $65bn+ business (used here, flagged as estimate).
  • Magnum Ice Cream Co.: demerged from Unilever 6 Dec 2025 (Unilever keeps ~19.9%); Unilever shown on continuing basis (€50.5bn). Magnum on its own (~€7.9bn) is well below the cut-off.
  • Ferrero + WK Kellogg: closed 26 Sep 2025, after Ferrero’s Aug-2025 year-end, so not yet in its €19.3bn figure.
  • Kimberly-Clark / Kenvue: still pending as of Oct 3, 2026 (shareholder & US HSR approvals done; foreign approvals outstanding; close expected Q4 2026). Separately: KMB $16.4bn + Kenvue $15.1bn (FY2025); combined ~$31.6bn would rank ~#17.
  • Marfrig + BRF merged into MBRF (2025). Kraft Heinz split paused Feb 2026, so shown as one company.

Excluded (commodity traders, co-ops, diversified)

ADM, Bunge, Cargill, Louis Dreyfus, COFCO, Wilmar, Olam: primarily agricultural commodity trading/processing. Cargill (est. ~$154bn total revenue, Forbes) does not disclose consumer-branded revenue. Dairy Farmers of America (~$23bn, 2024) and Fonterra are mostly raw-milk marketing/ingredient co-ops. Chinese hog producers (Muyuan, Wens) sell mainly live hogs. FEMSA and Uni-President are retail-dominated. Henkel (€20.5bn group) has only €9.7bn Consumer Brands sales.
