# Key findings: Big 10 food groups ownership (as of 3 Oct 2026, America/New_York)

1. **Mars now owns Kellanova (Kellogg's).** The deal closed **11 Dec 2025**, after the EU gave final approval on 8 Dec 2025. The price was $83.50 per share in cash (about $36bn), and Kellanova was delisted from the NYSE. Pringles, Cheez-It, Pop-Tarts, Rice Krispies Treats, RXBAR, Eggo, MorningStar Farms and Kellogg's international cereals now sit inside **Mars Snacking**. The map shows Kellanova as a sub-node under Mars; the Kellanova "group" entry is only a pointer to it.
2. **WK Kellogg Co is not part of Kellanova or Mars.** It holds Kellogg's cereal in the US, Canada and the Caribbean. It was spun off in Oct 2023, and **Ferrero bought it on 26 Sep 2025** for $23 per share (about $3.1bn).
3. **Unilever no longer owns its ice cream brands.** **The Magnum Ice Cream Company** was demerged on **6 Dec 2025** and listed in Amsterdam, London and New York on 8 Dec 2025. Magnum, Ben & Jerry's, Wall's, Cornetto, Breyers and the other ice cream brands belong to TMICC. Unilever keeps a **~19.85% minority stake** and plans to sell it within 5 years. TMICC also took **61.9% of Kwality Wall's (India)** on 30 Mar 2026. Ben & Jerry's is still at TMICC, which says the brand is "not for sale."
4. **Unilever is also separating its Foods business (PENDING).** On **31 Mar 2026** it agreed to combine Foods with **McCormick** (Knorr, Hellmann's, Marmite and others). Unilever and its shareholders would own 65% of the combined company, and Unilever would also get $15.7bn in cash. India/HUL, Portugal, Lipton RTD, Buavita and Liquid I.V. are excluded. The UK CMA has until 11 Nov 2026 to decide whether to open a Phase 2 review, and closing is expected by mid-2027. Separately, Unilever sold The Vegetarian Butcher to Vivera (Sep 2025). The Unox/Zwan sale to Zwanenberg was still under Dutch ACM review, and I could not confirm that it has closed.
5. **Nestlé is shrinking to four core businesses.** It **sold Blue Bottle** to Centurium (Apr 2026), sold its **last 40% of Herta** (Dec 2025) and **bought the remaining 51% of yfood** (Jul 2026). Three deals are pending:
   - **Waters** will move into **Peranel**, a 50:50 JV with Platinum Equity (expected H1 2027).
   - **Mainstream vitamins** (Nature's Bounty and others) will go to Yellow Wood Partners for about $1bn (expected H1 2027).
   - The remaining **ice cream** businesses are moving to **Froneri** in phases. Nestlé keeps its 50% of Froneri.
6. **PepsiCo has been buying "better-for-you" brands.** It took Sabra and Obela to 100% (Dec 2024) and bought **Siete** (Jan 2025) and **poppi** (May 2025). It raised its **Celsius** stake to about 11% and sold Rockstar's US/Canada rights to Celsius (Aug 2025). It still holds 39% of Tropicana Brands Group. Elliott has pushed for a Quaker sale, but PepsiCo has not committed to one.
7. **Coca-Cola kept Costa.** It ended the sale process in Jan 2026. It is selling down its bottlers: 40% of Hindustan Coca-Cola went to Jubilant Bhartia (Jul 2025), and control of **CCBA** is going to Coca-Cola HBC (pending, targeted for end-2026). BodyArmor and fairlife are 100% owned. Coca-Cola holds about 21% of Monster.
8. **General Mills is moving into pet food and out of dairy and Brazil.** It bought **Whitebridge** (Tiki Pets, Cloud Star) in Dec 2024. It sold Canadian yogurt to Sodiaal (Jan 2025) and US yogurt to Lactalis (Jun 2025), and **completed the sale of its Brazil business** (Yoki, Kitano) on 3 Sep 2026. The sale of its mainland-China Häagen-Dazs shops is pending.
9. **Danone is buying health and functional nutrition brands.** It took a **majority of Kate Farms** (Jul 2025) and completed the **Huel** acquisition (4 Sep 2026). It agreed to buy **MADE Group** and Saputo's 49% of their Australian JV (both pending), and sold its Lifeway stake (May 2026).
10. **ABF is consolidating UK bread and splitting off Primark.** It **bought Hovis** on 8 Jul 2026 and merged it with Allied Bakeries as Hovis Bakeries. A **Primark demerger** was announced in Apr 2026 and is expected in Dec 2027. **Mondelēz** made no new acquisitions in 2025–26. Its last moves were Evirth (majority, Nov 2024), selling its JDE Peet's stake (Nov 2024) and an approach to Hershey (Dec 2024) that led to no deal.
